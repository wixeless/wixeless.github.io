Privacy policy

How AdMobile – AdMob Metrics handles your information.

Overview

Timur Myrzaev ("developer", "we", "us") built AdMobile – AdMob Metrics ("app") as a freemium, ad-supported application with optional in-app subscriptions. This service is intended for use as is.

OAuth 2.0 scopes for Google APIs used in the app

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly lets the app view your AdMob reports and metrics.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adsense.readonly lets the app view your AdSense reports and metrics.

All data retrieved via Google APIs is accessed in read-only mode and processed locally on your device to display your dashboard and widgets. We do not store, sell, or transfer your AdMob or AdSense account data to external developer servers or third parties.

What information does the app obtain and how is it used?

Subscriptions and in-app purchases

The app offers optional paid subscriptions to unlock Pro features and remove advertisements. All payments and billing information are processed securely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details; we only receive a purchase token and subscription status from Google Play to enable Pro features. Please see Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles payment data.

Automatically collected information

In addition, the app may collect certain information automatically, such as the type of mobile device you use, your mobile device's unique advertising ID, the IP address of your mobile device, your mobile operating system, and information about the way you use the app. See the "Automatic data collection and advertising" section for examples.

If errors occur while using the app, an error log may be sent back to us. This error log contains non-personal diagnostic data, such as device model, operating system version, and where in the app the error happened. This information helps us improve stability and performance.

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device. Please see their privacy policies for further details:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob

Do third parties see or have access to information obtained by the app?

We will share information with third parties only in the ways described in this privacy policy. We may disclose automatically collected technical and diagnostic information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with trusted service providers who work on our behalf, do not have an independent use of the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to follow the rules set out in this privacy policy;
  • to third-party advertising networks and analytics companies, as described in the "Automatic data collection and advertising" section below.

Automatic data collection and advertising

We use third-party analytics and advertising SDKs (such as Firebase and Google AdMob) to understand app usage and display advertisements in the free version of the app. The types of data collected may include: (i) mobile device type and device properties; (ii) mobile operating system and app version; (iii) mobile carrier and approximate region; (iv) advertising identifiers; and (v) app interaction and diagnostic metrics.

We do not pass any personally identifiable information or Google API metrics to ad networks or advertisers. Where required by law (including in the EEA and UK), personalized advertising is shown only after obtaining your explicit consent.

Rights of users in the EEA and UK (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK), we process technical and advertising data based on your consent (for personalized ads) or our legitimate interests (for app functionality, analytics, and crash diagnostics). Because we do not maintain user accounts or store your personal data on our own servers, you can exercise your rights as follows:

  • delete locally stored app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, and revoke Google API access via your Google Account permissions;
  • opt out of personalized advertising or reset/delete your Advertising ID at any time through your Android device's privacy and ad settings;
  • request access to or deletion of any information you have sent us directly (such as support emails) by contacting wixelstore@gmail.com;
  • object to or restrict certain types of data processing, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Data retention and managing your information

Local app preferences and cached reports remain on your device for as long as you use the app and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account permissions. If you would like us to delete diagnostic or support data associated with your requests, please contact us at wixelstore@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This service does not address anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can take the necessary actions.

Security

We value your trust in using our app and apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted local storage for authentication tokens and HTTPS encryption in transit. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.
